How to play

Tap a piece value (1/2/3) below, then tap a cell. You have two of each value; your opponent too. You can overwrite opponent tiles with a strictly higher value; never overwrite your own.

  • Win with 3-in-a-row (by owner).
  • Inventory numbers are shown under each piece button.
  • Buttons disable when that value runs out.
